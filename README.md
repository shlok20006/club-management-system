Club Management System 🎉

A smart and interactive Club Management System developed to simplify club handling for both students and college management.

This platform helps students discover clubs and events based on their interests while enabling college authorities to efficiently manage club activities, achievements, and communication.

---

📌 Problem Statement

In many colleges, students face difficulty choosing the right clubs and staying updated about events. Managing multiple clubs, activities, and achievements is also challenging for college administration.

To solve this issue, we developed a centralized platform that improves communication, engagement, and club management.

---

🚀 Features

- 💬 Real-time Chat System
- 🎯 Club & Event Suggestions Based on Interests
- 🏆 Club Achievement Showcase
- 📅 Event Management
- 👥 Student & Club Interaction
- 🔐 User Authentication System
- 📊 Organized Club Information
